Privacy Charter & Patron Confidentiality

Last Updated: February 2026 • Strict adherence to European GDPR, French CNIL, and international data protection regulations.

1. Commitment to Absolute Patron Confidentiality

At IvorySatchelRidge Atelier Privé ("we", "our", "the Atelier"), we hold the confidentiality and personal dignity of our patrons in the highest esteem. Whether you commission a bespoke satchel, inquire about leather restoration, or subscribe to our quarterly research monographs, we handle your personal information with the same rigorous care and discretion we apply to our fine leathercraft.

This Privacy Charter sets forth the principles, legal bases, and security safeguards governing the collection, processing, storage, and erasure of personal data acquired through our website (ivorysatchelridge.com) and during private bespoke consultations.

2. Information We Collect and Receive

We collect only the minimum personal data strictly necessary to fulfill our bespoke artisan obligations:

  • Patron Identification Data: Full name, title, preferred contact telephone, and private email address.
  • Delivery and Physical Logistics Data: Residential or diplomatic shipping address, courier access instructions, and customs clearance identifiers for insured white-glove transit.
  • Bespoke Commission Specifications: Custom dimension preferences, laptop model tolerances, debossing initials, selected hide swatches, and leathercraft customization records.
  • Technical and Telemetry Data: IP address, device operating system, browser configuration, language settings, and aggregated page navigation metrics captured via Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-0LY0HY7L01).

3. Legal Bases for Data Processing (GDPR Article 6)

We process your personal information strictly under one or more of the following lawful grounds:

  • Contractual Performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): Executing bespoke craftsmanship contracts, procuring specific hides, scheduling bench hours, and delivering finished commissions.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Maintaining our lifetime restoration registry, verifying warranty claims, and safeguarding website security against unauthorized intrusions.
  • Legal Compliance (Art. 6(1)(c)): Retaining financial tax records, customs import/export manifests, and invoicing ledgers as required by French commercial law.
  • Explicit Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Sending our educational research treatises and quarterly atelier dispatches, which you may revoke at any time.

4. Lifetime Heirloom Registry and Data Retention

To uphold our lifetime restoration and restitching guarantee, IvorySatchelRidge maintains an encrypted, air-gapped Master Ledger of Commissions. This registry records the serial number of your bag, the date of completion, the master artisan assigned, the exact hide batch, and the Lin Câblé thread specification.

This provenance data is preserved indefinitely to ensure that future conservators or generational heirs can verify authenticity and restitch seams with exact archival materials. If you prefer your commission details to be anonymized or erased from the public registry, you may submit an erasure request at any time.

5. Third-Party Disclosures and Zero Data Commercialization

We unequivocally state: IvorySatchelRidge never sells, rents, leases, or trades patron personal data to any third-party marketing brokers, data aggregators, or commercial advertisers. We share data solely with vetted enterprise service providers under binding confidentiality agreements:

  • Insured International Couriers: DHL Express, FedEx Custom Critical, and specialized armored couriers for high-value dispatch.
  • Secure Payment Processors: PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways handling tokenized transactions with zero plain-text card retention.
  • Analytical Infrastructure: Google LLC for aggregated, anonymized web performance insights.

6. Your Sovereign Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under international privacy frameworks, you retain comprehensive rights over your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request a complete copy of all personal records held within our archives.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct any inaccuracies in your shipping or contact information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request the complete deletion of your records, subject to statutory tax retention mandates.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain your bespoke commission specifications in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we process your information during active disputes.

8. International Data Transfers and Cross-Border Protections

Because IvorySatchelRidge serves discerning patrons across Europe, North America, Asia, and the Middle East, personal shipping information may be transferred across international borders solely to facilitate customs clearance and insured courier dispatch. All cross-border data transfers adhere strictly to standard contractual clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, ensuring that your data receives identical legal protections regardless of destination country.

9. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling Exclusions

We do not employ automated algorithms, artificial intelligence profiling engines, or predictive scoring models to make decisions concerning your bespoke commission eligibility, pricing, or patron relationship. Every interaction within our atelier is conducted directly by human artisans and dedicated concierge professionals.

10. Cookie Governance and Analytics Opt-Out

Patrons may configure their browser settings at any time to block performance cookies or opt out of Google Analytics measurement. For complete details regarding our minimal cookie usage, please consult our dedicated Cookie Policy.

11. Data Breach Incident Notification Protocols

In the highly improbable event of a security incident affecting personal patron information, IvorySatchelRidge maintains a formal Incident Response Protocol adhering strictly to GDPR Article 33. We will notify the French Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés (CNIL) within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of any breach presenting risk to patron rights, and communicate directly with affected individuals without undue delay.

12. Special Categories of Data and Minor Protection

IvorySatchelRidge does not solicit, collect, or process special categories of sensitive personal data (such as biometric identifiers, philosophical affiliations, or health data). Our bespoke maroquinerie services are directed exclusively to adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from minors without verifiable parental consent.

13. Archival Governance and Ledger Security

Physical commission ledgers stored in our Paris archive are housed within fire-rated, climate-stabilized vault storage. Access is restricted exclusively to senior master artisans and sworn conservatory staff to ensure permanent patron confidentiality across generations.

14. Regular Privacy Audits and Governance Oversight

IvorySatchelRidge conducts comprehensive semi-annual privacy and data governance audits to verify compliance with evolving European and international cybersecurity standards. Our systems are routinely tested for vulnerability mitigation, encrypted backup validation, and strict administrative role segregation to safeguard our patrons' personal records against any unauthorized access.

7. Data Security Safeguards and Contact

All digital transmissions across our platform are secured using 256-bit TLS encryption protocols. Digital databases are protected by multi-factor authentication, enterprise firewalls, and role-based administrative permissions.

For questions regarding this charter, or to exercise your privacy rights, please contact our Data Governance Officer at: privacy@ivorysatchelridge.com or write to IvorySatchelRidge Data Governance, 74 Rue du Faubourg Saint-Antoine, 75012 Paris, France.
