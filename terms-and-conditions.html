Bespoke Commission Protocols & Retainers

Each astronomical regulator and wristwatch is an individual work of micro-mechanical sculpture engineered to client specifications. Commissions commence upon receipt of a 50% non-refundable raw materials deposit, which secures designated noble metals, synthetic ruby jewels, and Invar 36 alloy stock.

Natural Material Variations & Artisanal Authenticity

Because each dial is hand-turned on antique rose-engine lathes and casework is hand-polished, minor microscopic variations in guilloché refractive luster and hand-anglage reflections are inherent hallmarks of authentic artisan craft. These variations confirm genuine human craftsmanship and do not constitute mechanical flaws.
