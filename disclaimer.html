Mechanical Rate Accuracy & Environmental Notice

While Pendulum Craft regulators and wristwatches are engineered to astronomical observatory tolerances (±0.2 seconds per day under controlled conditions), mechanical timekeeping is influenced by gravitational orientation, local barometric pressure, and ambient temperature gradients. Stated performance metrics reflect calibrated benchmark testing.

Magnetic Field Precautions

While our escapements utilize antimagnetic beryllium-copper and Invar alloys, extreme magnetic fields (exceeding 4,800 A/m, such as magnetic resonance imaging scanners or high-output audio transformers) may magnetize steel pinions, requiring demagnetization at our atelier.
