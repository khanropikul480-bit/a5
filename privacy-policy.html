Privacy Policy

Ethical standards and operational transparency at Pendulum Craft.

At Pendulum Craft ("we", "our", "us"), accessible from https://pendulumcraft.com, the confidentiality and data integrity of our private collectors, diplomatic clients, and web visitors is held in the highest regard. This Privacy Policy details our operational data handling protocols.

Confidential Client Information We Collect

When you commission a bespoke astronomical regulator, reserve an atelier bench consultation, or contact our master horologists, we collect personal contact information provided voluntarily. This includes your full name, styling title, delivery residence, telephone contact, and official electronic mail address.

Payment transactions, banking deposits, and wire transfer documents are processed through SOC-2 certified, encrypted private banking channels; no complete financial account credentials are stored on local servers.

Application of Collector Records

Client information is utilized strictly to fulfill custom timepiece commissions, coordinate insured armored transit, issue official observatory chronometer certificates, and provide lifetime maintenance alerts. We observe strict discretion and never disclose collector identities or collection values to third parties.

Server Logs and Digital Telemetry

Pendulum Craft maintains standard server log records. These files document visitors during site interaction, noting IP addresses, browser specifications, Internet Service Providers (ISPs), date and time coordinates, referring pages, and user navigation paths to optimize page delivery and accessibility.

Client Data Rights

Under international data protection regulations, clients may inspect, update, or request deletion of stored contact archives at any time. Direct your requests to our private concierge at atelier@pendulumcraft.com or visit us at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.
