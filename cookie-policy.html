Cookie Policy

Ethical standards and operational transparency at Pendulum Craft.

This Cookie Policy explains how Pendulum Craft utilizes cookies and related telemetry tools when you browse https://pendulumcraft.com.

Understanding Digital Cookies

Cookies are minute alphanumeric data packets stored on your computing hardware by web browsers. They permit our site to maintain session state, remember commission selections, and deliver smooth high-resolution imagery.

Types of Cookies Deployed

  • Strictly Essential Cookies: Indispensable for navigational security, form submission, and CSRF protection.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Integrated through Google Analytics Tag (G-0LY0HY7L01) to evaluate aggregated traffic, popular research articles, and site speed.
  • Functional Preference Cookies: Save user display choices and interactive complication configurator states.

Managing Cookie Configurations

You may adjust cookie permissions through your web browser preferences at any time. Disabling analytical cookies will not restrict your access to our bespoke inquiry portals or published monographs.

Updates to This Protocol

We may periodically update this Cookie Policy to reflect technical refinements across our web architecture. Continued use indicates your informed consent.
