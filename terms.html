Terms of Commission & Atelier Privé Charter

Effective Date: February 2026 • Governing all bespoke handcrafted orders, hide reservations, and lifetime restoration services.

1. Scope and Artisan Lineage

These Terms of Commission govern all bespoke orders, private consultations, hide swatch requests, and restoration services executed by IvorySatchelRidge Atelier Privé. By submitting a commission inquiry, approving a pattern blueprint, or remitting a production deposit, the patron agrees to be bound by these provisions in full.

Every item crafted by our atelier is executed individually by a dedicated master leatherworker without automated assembly lines. Because natural full-grain leather is an organic material, subtle variations in grain texture, growth wrinkles, and botanical dye absorption are intrinsic hallmarks of authentic artisanal creation rather than defects.

2. Commission Process, Deposits, and Payment Schedules

The commissioning of an IvorySatchelRidge heirloom follows a formal four-stage financial schedule:

  • Stage 1: Consultation & Specification: Preliminary design reviews, leather swatch selections, and dimension approvals are conducted at no charge.
  • Stage 2: Bench Reservation Deposit (50%): Upon written approval of the architectural specification, a non-refundable deposit of fifty percent (50%) is required to allocate specific hide inventory and reserve 35+ bench hours for your master artisan.
  • Stage 3: Bench Construction & Progress Dossier: Your master artisan executes pattern drafting, hand skiving, two-needle saddle stitching, and edge burnishing. Photographic progress updates are provided upon request.
  • Stage 4: Final Balance & Dispatch (50%): Upon final quality inspection and photographic sign-off, the remaining fifty percent (50%) balance plus any applicable local duties is due prior to insured international dispatch.

3. Production Timelines and Handcraft Lead Times

Standard bespoke commissions require an estimated production duration of six (6) to eight (8) weeks from receipt of the deposit. Complex bespoke architectures featuring custom sand-cast lock molds or exotic vegetable hides may require ten (10) to twelve (12) weeks.

Because our craft relies upon unhurried artisan precision, drying times for multi-layer edge burnishes, and natural pit tanning schedules, lead times are reasonable estimations rather than rigid contractual guarantees. We prioritize flawless structural integrity over rushed delivery.

4. Lifetime Restoration Guarantee and Warranty Policy

IvorySatchelRidge warrants that every bag leaving our bench is free from defects in materials and master workmanship. We provide a Lifetime Structural Restoration Guarantee covering:

  • Complimentary restitching of any Lin Câblé saddle seam damaged during normal active use.
  • Repair or replacement of sand-cast solid brass or palladium hardware, locks, and springs.
  • Complimentary annual cleaning, edge inspection, and organic beeswax nourishment at our Paris atelier (patron responsible for round-trip insured transit).

This warranty excludes damages resulting from deliberate abuse, sharp blade incisions, chemical solvent spills, improper radiator heat drying, or repairs attempted by unauthorized third parties.

5. Custom Commission Cancellation & Return Policy

Pursuant to Article L221-28 of the French Consumer Code and international commercial jurisprudence, bespoke goods crafted to specific personalized dimensions, initials, or unique hide selections are exempt from standard statutory withdrawal rights.

Once leather cutting and skiving have commenced, the initial 50% production deposit is entirely non-refundable. If you wish to cancel an order prior to hide cutting, a cancellation fee of 15% will be deducted to cover administrative pattern drafting and swatch curation expenses.

7. Force Majeure and Unforeseeable Delays

Neither party shall be liable for delays resulting from causes beyond reasonable control, including extreme weather disrupting Alpine tannery drying cycles, disruptions in international insured transport networks, or official governmental customs embargoes. In such events, IvorySatchelRidge will promptly notify the patron and provide an adjusted delivery schedule.

8. Bespoke Customization Approvals & Client Sign-Off

Prior to cutting precious full-grain hides, the patron will receive a formal Commission Dossier detailing outer dimensions, internal pocket configurations, leather colorways, Lin Câblé thread codes, and hardware finishes. Written approval (digital or signed) of this dossier constitutes the final binding agreement on specifications. Any alterations requested after hide skiving has commenced may incur additional material and labor fees.

9. Severability and Complete Agreement

If any provision of these Terms is declared invalid or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. These Terms constitute the entire understanding between IvorySatchelRidge and the patron, superseding all prior informal discussions.

10. Customs, Duties, and International Import Taxes

For commissions shipped outside the European Union, the patron remains the designated importer of record and is responsible for all applicable local import tariffs, value-added taxes (VAT), and customs clearance fees imposed by the destination country. IvorySatchelRidge provides accurate commercial invoices and certificates of French artisanal origin to facilitate smooth customs processing.

11. Risk of Loss and Title Transfer

Title and risk of loss for finished commissions pass to the patron upon physical delivery and signature receipt from our designated insured express courier. In the event of transit loss or catastrophic package damage, IvorySatchelRidge will immediately prioritize bench hours to craft an identical replacement piece at zero additional cost to the patron.

12. Mediation and Amicable Dispute Resolution

In the spirit of classical artisanal fellowship, the parties agree that in the event of any disagreement or dispute arising under these Terms, they will first engage in good-faith private mediation through the Centre de Médiation et d'Arbitrage de Paris (CMAP) prior to initiating formal judicial proceedings.

13. Electronic Signature and Digital Communications Consent

The parties acknowledge and agree that electronic approvals, digital signatures, and confirmed email communications shall be considered legally binding and admissible in any legal or administrative proceedings, possessing identical legal validity as physically signed paper documents under French Civil Code Article 1366.

6. Intellectual Property & Governing Law

All proprietary pattern geometries, hardware blueprints, brand crests, photography, and monograph texts remain the exclusive intellectual property of IvorySatchelRidge. These Terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of France, with exclusive jurisdiction resting in the Commercial Courts of Paris.
