Digital Governance

Cookie Policy & Tracking Preferences

Clear transparency on how we utilize minimal digital cookies and telemetry to enhance your digital atelier experience.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are compact text files placed upon your computing device or mobile terminal when navigating digital platforms. They serve essential functions such as preserving your visual theme preference (Light / Dark mode), remembering items in your leather configurator preview, and providing anonymous telemetry regarding site performance.

IvorySatchelRidge maintains an austere, privacy-first digital architecture. We do not utilize intrusive cross-site tracking cookies, behavioral ad retargeting pixels, or third-party data harvesting scripts.

2. Categories of Cookies We Employ

We classify digital cookies across three defined functional categories:

  • Strictly Necessary & Functional Cookies: Essential for fundamental platform operations, including CSRF security tokens, mobile navigation state preservation, and visual theme toggling (stored locally as ivorysatchel_theme). These cannot be deactivated without disrupting site functionality.
  • Analytical & Performance Cookies: Operated via Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) using cookies such as _ga and _ga_0LY0HY7L01. These cookies capture aggregated, anonymized metrics (page response latency, geographic country of origin, bounce rates) to assist us in optimizing server infrastructure. All IP addresses are automatically anonymized.
  • Artisan Configurator Preference Cookies: Temporary session cookies that preserve your bespoke hide, thread, and hardware selections while you navigate between our atelier catalogue and private consultation request forms.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookie Preferences

You maintain sovereign control over cookie storage. You may adjust your web browser preferences to refuse all non-essential cookies, alert you when a cookie is issued, or purge existing cookies from your browser cache.

To adjust cookie settings in standard browsers:

  • Safari: Preferences › Privacy › Manage Website Data.
  • Google Chrome: Settings › Privacy and Security › Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings › Cookies and site permissions › Manage and delete cookies.

6. Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Signals

Our website respects Do Not Track (DNT) signals and Global Privacy Control (GPC) headers transmitted by your web browser. When such signals are detected, our systems automatically suppress all non-essential analytical scripts and telemetry cookies, ensuring that your browsing session remains completely anonymous.

7. Local Storage and Session Token Security

We utilize HTML5 Local Storage solely to remember your chosen visual theme (stored as ivorysatchel_theme) across page loads so that you do not experience visual flashes when navigating between monographs. This token contains zero identifying personal information, is stored strictly on your local device, and is never transmitted to our remote servers.

8. Telemetry Anonymization & Zero IP Storage

All analytics traffic processed through our Google Analytics integration operates with strict IP masking enabled (anonymizeIp = true). The last octet of your IPv4 address and the last 80 bits of your IPv6 address are automatically set to zeroes before storage, ensuring that your geographic data can never be resolved to an individual street address or identifiable subscriber identity.

9. Cookie Consent Verification Protocol

When you select your theme or configure custom bag specifications on our platform, our local storage scripts record a cryptographic hash of your session state. This token expires automatically after twelve months of inactivity, or immediately upon manual browser cache clearing.

5. Inquiries and Policy Updates

We may periodically revise this Cookie Policy to reflect technical updates or regulatory guidance. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated revision date. For questions regarding our digital privacy practices, please contact: concierge@ivorysatchelridge.com.
