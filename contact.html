Bespoke Concierge Protocols

What to Expect During Your Private Consultation

A comprehensive step-by-step overview of our individualized patron consultation experience.

Step A

Initial Lifestyle & Ergonomic Assessment

We review your daily travel routines, laptop dimensions, document volume, and shoulder strap preferences to engineer optimal internal volume and weight distribution.

Step B

Tactile Hide Swatch Evaluation

We dispatch our curated leather swatch box to your residence, allowing you to experience the natural scent, grain density, and colorways of our pit-tanned hides under natural daylight.

Step C

Architectural Blueprint & Bench Scheduling

Your master artisan prepares a custom zinc pattern schematic, locks in your hardware casting specifications, and establishes your dedicated bench allocation.

Atelier Privé Protocol

Private Appointment Guidelines for International Patrons

Ensuring an intimate, unhurried consultation experience at our Paris atelier.

🕑

Dedicated Artisan Session

Each private consultation is allocated a minimum of two uninterrupted hours with our Master Saddler and Chief Bag Architect to ensure thorough review of all pattern schematics.

👐

Natural Light Swatch Review

Our Paris consultation salon features expansive northern skylights, allowing you to examine full hide pelts under true neutral daylight to evaluate natural patina potential.

🎨

Live Bench Demonstrations

During your visit, observe live demonstrations of two-needle saddle stitching, hot brass iron creasing, and hand-burnishing techniques at our active artisan benches.

Our private concierge is at your full disposal to facilitate telephone, video, or in-person atelier appointments. Whether commissioning a bespoke satchel or requesting restoration on an antique heirloom, we ensure dedicated attention to every detail.
